Set up your app on Meta

do this once

You said your Meta app and number are ready. These are the three things the app needs from it — all on one screen: developers.facebook.com → your app → WhatsApp → API Setup.

  1. Phone number ID — shown under your number on that page. Not the phone number itself.
  2. WhatsApp Business Account ID — same page, just below.
  3. A permanent token. The token on that page dies after 24 hours. Make a lasting one: business.facebook.com → Business settings → Users → System users → Add → Assign assets twice: your app and your WhatsApp account, both Full control → Generate new token → expiry Never → tick whatsapp_business_messaging + whatsapp_business_management Forgetting the WhatsApp account half is why most tokens fail.

Create your promo template on Meta

waiting

This is the part people miss. To message a client who hasn't written to you in the last 24 hours, WhatsApp only allows a template Meta approved in advance. You write it once, Meta reviews it (usually minutes), then you can send it to your whole list.

business.facebook.com → WhatsApp Manager → Message templates → Create template → category Marketing → pick your language → write the text

Use {{1}} where the client's name goes, e.g.
Bonjour {{1}}, -20% sur tout le mois d'août chez nous. Répondez STOP pour ne plus recevoir nos offres.
Adding a STOP line is the single cheapest thing you can do to protect your number — it turns an angry Report into a harmless reply this app handles for you.
