Set up your app on Meta

do this once

Three things from developers.facebook.com → your app → WhatsApp → API Setup.

  1. Phone number ID — shown under your number. Not the phone number itself.
  2. WhatsApp Business Account ID — same page, just below.
  3. A permanent token. The one on that page dies after 24 hours. business.facebook.com → Business settings → Users → System users → Add → assign assets twice: your app and your WhatsApp account, both Full control → Generate new token → expiry Never → tick whatsapp_business_messaging + whatsapp_business_management Forgetting the WhatsApp account half is why most tokens fail.

Full walkthrough: SETUP.md

Create your promo template

waiting

To message a client who hasn't written in 24 hours, WhatsApp only allows a template Meta approved in advance. You write it once, Meta reviews it, then you can send it to your whole list.

business.facebook.com → WhatsApp Manager → Message templates → Create template → category Marketing → pick your language → write the text

Put {{1}} where the client's name goes:

Bonjour {{1}}, -20% sur toute la collection jusqu'au 31 août chez [votre boutique].
Répondez STOP pour ne plus recevoir nos offres.

A STOP line is the cheapest protection there is — it turns an angry Report into a reply this app handles for you.
